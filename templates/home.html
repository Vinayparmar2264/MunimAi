What We Fix

3 things that cost retailers money every day

Running out of stock

When popular items sell out, you lose customers and revenue. MunimAI tells you exactly when and how much to reorder.

Products expiring unsold

Stock that expires before it sells is pure loss. We compare your sales speed to expiry date and recommend the right discount at the right time.

Wrong pricing decisions

Discount too early and lose profit. Discount too late and waste stock. Our AI finds the perfect price using real demand data.
